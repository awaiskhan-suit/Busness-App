# Busness-App
I build business app for shopping mart,for simple shop,online business and for whole company .
In this app first of all you see a dashboard having following information like how many products, how many employees, total sales, today sales, expanses. in this app we add employee detail and we can also edit, delete employee record and pdf of employee record is also generated. In this app we also add products and we can also edit and delete the products. We can also generate invoice and when we generate invoice so that invoice can be share by whattsapp. When we enter quantity of product in invoice so stock quantity of this product is also change which is show by message or in text form. We can also see the sales of specific date and we can also add expenses and we can also edit and delete expenses
